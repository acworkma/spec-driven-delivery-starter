# Retro-spec drill (40 minutes)

**Why do it?** Re-writing an epic you have already delivered as a spec is the fastest way to see what spec-driven delivery changes. You already know the answers, and when you learned them. The gap count becomes your first baseline.

**What to bring:** one recently delivered epic, with its description, stories and acceptance criteria as originally written. No PHI, member data or real names.

**Worked example:** `retro-spec/vision-rider-2025/`.

| Minutes | Step |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Read the original epic aloud. Agree what was actually delivered. |
| 5–20 | Re-spec it with the `retro-spec` skill, or with `/speckit-specify` followed by `/speckit-clarify`. Answer every clarifying question with what you eventually learned. |
| 20–35 | Fill in the gap log below. For each gap, when did the team actually find out? |
| 35–40 | Summarize it in three lines for the readout. |
